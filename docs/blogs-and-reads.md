## 💡 Tips for Learning

:::tip "How to Stay Updated"
    1. **Follow key researchers** on Twitter/X and read their blogs
    2. **Subscribe to newsletters**: Import AI, The Batch (DeepLearning.AI)
    3. **Check arXiv** daily for new papers in your area of interest
    4. **Participate in Kaggle competitions** for hands-on experience
    5. **Join ML communities** on Discord, Reddit, or LinkedIn
    6. **Read code**: Study implementations on GitHub
    7. **Build projects**: Apply what you learn to real problems

:::note "Remember"
    The field moves fast! Bookmark resources, take notes, and revisit fundamentals regularly.
